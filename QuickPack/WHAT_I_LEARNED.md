3. **Separate Applications:** Develop separate applications for Customers and Warehouse owners.
4. **Multiple Products:** Allow customers to browse and purchase multiple products.
5. **Offers and Discounts:** Add offer cards, discount coupons and special deals.
6. **Reward Coins:** Introduce reward coins for purchases and customer activities.
7. **Product Reviews:** Allow customers to give ratings and reviews.

11. **Reports and Analytics:** Generate sales, inventory and customer reports.

### Final Goal

My goal is to make QuickPack a complete, secure and user-friendly platform with separate Customer and Warehouse applications, smart product management, online payments and a better shopping experience.
